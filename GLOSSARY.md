# Tutor

Tutor supports learning from a learner's own study materials and evidence of understanding.

## Language

**Local-first**:
Study materials and learning history are owned and persisted locally. Relevant context may be sent to a connected AI provider for a tutoring interaction with clear disclosure; this does not mean inference runs locally.

**Activity**:
Work a learner undertakes, such as reading a passage or completing an exercise. Completion does not itself demonstrate mastery of a learning goal.
_Avoid_: Moment

**Learning goal**:
Observable knowledge or skill a learner aims to demonstrate, such as distinguishing nominative and accusative in an unseen sentence.

**Source conflict**:
A disagreement between study materials relevant to an explanation or assessment. Tutor surfaces and cites both passages, explains possible interpretations, and keeps affected assessment uncertain until the conflict is clarified.

**Disputed assessment**:
An assessment challenged by the learner whose source-based review remains inconclusive. It leaves the prior knowledge state unchanged while further evidence may be gathered.

**Assisted practice**:
Practice where the learner has received help, including a revealed solution. The recorded evidence distinguishes that help from independent demonstration of understanding.

**Simulated ChatGPT**:
An explicitly labeled development/test substitute that provides scripted connection states and tutor responses without a real account or provider traffic. Its results verify Tutor's behavior and do not establish live authentication, subscription access or OS credential protection.
